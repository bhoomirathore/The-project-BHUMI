# B.H.U.M.I Operational Workflow

## 1. Land Verification & Title Lookup
- Citizen enters Khasra Number or ULPIN (Unique Land Parcel Identification Number).
- System checks the Hyperledger immutable ledger for title ownership, encumbrance history, and drone GIS boundary logs.

## 2. Appointment & eKYC Processing
- Buyer/Seller schedule an appointment slot at the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO).
- Officer verifies biometrics via Aadhaar eKYC facial recognition scan.

## 3. Smart Contract Mutation & Block Commitment
- Officer executes the smart contract on the blockchain.
- The land title is mutated to the new owner, creating a new block in the Hyperledger network.
- Digital E-Deed certificate is minted with a cryptographic QR code seal.
